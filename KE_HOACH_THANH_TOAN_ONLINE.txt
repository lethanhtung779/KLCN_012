KE HOACH TRIEN KHAI CHUC NANG THANH TOAN ONLINE

1. PHUONG AN DE XUAT

- Su dung payOS Hosted Checkout va VietQR.
- Tien thanh toan chuyen vao tai khoan ngan hang lien ket voi payOS.
- Web chi hien thi giao dien mua goi va ket qua thanh toan.
- API tao don, lam viec voi payOS va xac minh webhook.
- SQL Server luu goi VIP, don thanh toan va lich su xu ly.
- WinForms dung de quan tri, doi soat giao dich va cap VIP thu cong khi can.

2. LUONG THANH TOAN

1) Hoc vien dang nhap va chon goi VIP.
2) Web goi API de tao don thanh toan.
3) API lay gia goi tu database, luu don o trang thai Cho thanh toan.
4) API goi payOS de tao lien ket thanh toan va ma QR.
5) Hoc vien thanh toan bang ung dung ngan hang.
6) payOS gui webhook ve API.
7) API kiem tra chu ky, ma don, so tien va trang thai.
8) Neu hop le, API cap VIP cho hoc vien va danh dau don Da thanh toan.
9) He thong gui email xac nhan va hien thi ket qua cho hoc vien.

Luu y: returnUrl chi dung de hien thi ket qua. He thong chi cap VIP sau khi
xac minh webhook hop le tu payOS.

3. QUY TAC NGHIEP VU

- Goi ban dau de xuat: VIP 30 ngay.
- Gia goi do quan tri vien cau hinh.
- VIP bat dau tu luc thanh toan thanh cong.
- Neu hoc vien dang con VIP, so ngay mua them duoc cong vao ngay het han.
- Moi don thanh toan chi duoc cap VIP mot lan.
- Don chua thanh toan khong duoc thay doi quyen loi tai khoan.
- Quan tri vien co the cap VIP thu cong nhung phai nhap ly do va luu lich su.

5. API CAN XAY DUNG

- GET  /api/vip/packages
- POST /api/payments/payos
- GET  /api/payments/{id}
- GET  /api/payments/my-history
- POST /api/payments/payos/webhook
- POST /api/payments/{id}/cancel

Hoc vien chi duoc xem giao dich cua minh. Quan tri vien duoc xem tat ca.
So tien phai do API lay tu database, khong tin so tien gui tu trinh duyet.

6. GIAO DIEN WEB

- Trang Nang cap VIP.
- Danh sach cac goi VIP va quyen loi.
- Nut Thanh toan.
- Trang hien thi ma QR hoac chuyen den payOS.
- Trang ket qua: dang xu ly, thanh cong, that bai, da huy.
- Trang lich su thanh toan.
- Hien thi ngay het han VIP tren thong tin tai khoan.

7. WINFORMS QUAN TRI

- Them muc Thanh toan va VIP.
- Xem danh sach giao dich.
- Loc theo hoc vien, thoi gian va trang thai.
- Xem so tien, ma don va ma tham chieu ngan hang.
- Doi soat don dang cho thanh toan.
- Xem lich su cap VIP.
- Cap VIP thu cong kem ly do.
- Khong cho sua truc tiep giao dich da thanh toan.

8. BAO MAT

- Client ID, API Key va Checksum Key chi dat trong API.
- Khong dua khoa payOS vao Web, JavaScript hoac GitHub.
- Kiem tra chu ky HMAC cua webhook.
- Doi chieu OrderCode, so tien va trang thai.
- Dung transaction SQL khi cap VIP va cap nhat giao dich.
- Tao rang buoc duy nhat de tranh xu ly mot giao dich nhieu lan.
- Khong ghi API key vao log.
- Gioi han so lan tao don lien tuc.

9. YEU CAU TRIEN KHAI

- API webhook phai co dia chi HTTPS cong khai va on dinh.
- Khi chay tren may ca nhan co the dung Cloudflare Tunnel de demo.
- Khong nen dung URL tunnel tam thoi cho thanh toan that lau dai.
- Khi trien khai chinh thuc, API can duoc dat tren hosting co HTTPS.
- payOS hien khong co sandbox rieng, vi vay chi thu giao dich that voi so tien nho
  sau khi da hoan thanh kiem thu tu dong.

10. CAC TRUONG HOP CAN KIEM THU

- Thanh toan thanh cong.
- Nguoi dung huy thanh toan.
- Don het han.
- Webhook bi gui lap lai.
- Webhook sai chu ky.
- So tien webhook khong khop.
- Webhook den truoc hoac sau khi trinh duyet quay ve.
- Nguoi dung bam tao thanh toan nhieu lan.
- Nguoi dung co truy cap giao dich cua nguoi khac.
- API mat ket noi trong luc xu ly.
- Hoc vien dang co VIP mua them goi.
- Tien da ve ngan hang nhung webhook bi gian doan.
- Quan tri vien cap VIP thu cong.

11. THU TU TRIEN KHAI

1) Chot gia va thoi han goi VIP.
2) Tao tai khoan payOS, xac minh va lien ket ngan hang.
3) Cap nhat database va file SQL.
4) Xay dung service tich hop payOS.
5) Lam API tao don va webhook.
6) Lam giao dien mua VIP tren Web.
7) Lam man hinh quan ly giao dich tren WinForms.
8) Viet kiem thu chu ky, trang thai va chong xu ly trung.
9) Mo HTTPS hoac tunnel va dang ky webhook.
10) Thu giao dich that voi so tien nho.
11) Doi chieu tien vao tai khoan ngan hang truoc khi dua vao su dung.

12. THONG TIN CAN CHOT TRUOC KHI LAP TRINH

- Gia goi VIP.
- Thoi han goi VIP.
- Tai khoan ngan hang se lien ket voi payOS.
- Co cho phep mua nhieu goi de cong don thoi han hay khong.
- Chinh sach huy hoac hoan tien.
